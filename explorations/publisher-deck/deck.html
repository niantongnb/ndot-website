§ 01 The problem · The price
The price

The market has already priced this in

Investors are discounting media businesses that don't own their audience. Traffic-dependent valuations have compressed sharply as search and social referral has become unreliable.

Before
~10x

Revenue multiple, traffic-dependent media.

Compression
40 to 60%
After
4to6x

Revenue multiple, once search referral craters.

Correction

An earlier reading of traffic-dependent media valued those businesses at roughly 10x revenue. Following the collapse in search referral, the figure now clearing the market is 4 to 6x. The multiple was never the asset. The audience was.

Filed under: things that get re-rated before they get fixed
Owning your audience isn't just a growth strategy. It's a hedge against how the market is already re-pricing this risk.
