Design directions

Two directions for the NDot AI site, three layouts each.

Identical copy and identical brand rules across all six. The direction decides how much of the argument is carried by type and how much by graphics. The round is a different page geometry, not a restyling of the same one: the grid, what is pinned, and how the page advances all change. Every version is a working HTML mockup, not a picture.

Inside any version, the bar in the bottom-right corner switches direction and round without coming back here.

Round 1 of each direction is the original stacked page. Rounds 2 and 3 are rebuilds, not restyles.
