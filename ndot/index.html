NDot AI

Three directions

Three directions for the NDot AI site.

Identical copy, identical structure, identical brand rules across all three. Each takes its design language from a different reference site, named under the title. Every version is a working HTML mockup, not a picture. NDot AI here is the B2B growth platform for media companies, not the consumer local-life product. That one is Nearby, and it lives at the root of this site.

The directions

01

Institutional

after hebbia.com

Cinematic dark. High contrast serif set in caps, warm near black ground, one blue accent, dense prospectus type. Sells gravitas before it sells a feature.

Home Careers
02

Console

after exa.ai

Light and engineered. Warm flare serif over a grotesk, monospace micro labels, hairline rules, small radii, and a working surface in the hero rather than a picture of one.

Home Careers
03

Lab

after a1base.com

A research lab. Amber on near black against cream, square corners throughout, tiny wide tracked mono labels over large tightly set serif, and a key value specimen strip.
