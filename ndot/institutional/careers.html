Why now

The model is breaking

For years, publishers have depended heavily on search, social platforms, and other third-party channels for distribution and growth. As referral traffic continues to decline in the AI era, that model is becoming increasingly unpredictable. Building direct audience relationships — and the capabilities to grow, engage, retain, and monetize them — is becoming mission-critical.

That's what we're building at NDot AI.

What we bring

Capabilities, validated in operation

NDot AI brings together more than a decade of proven capabilities across app and newsletter development, audience and subscriber growth, recommendation and personalization, engagement and retention, advertising technology, content moderation, and publisher-advertiser networks — all developed and repeatedly validated through operating media products at scale.

We've built and scaled products serving tens of millions of users and generating hundreds of millions in revenue. Now, we're turning that experience into an integrated growth platform for media companies — helping publishers transform strong content and trusted brands into owned audiences, deeper relationships, and sustainable revenue.

The work

End to end, across an industry

This is a rare opportunity to work across product, growth, data, recommendation systems, advertising technology, and media — solving complex problems end to end and building technology that can reshape how an entire industry grows.

If you want to help media companies take control of their audience, growth, and future, come build NDot AI with us.
