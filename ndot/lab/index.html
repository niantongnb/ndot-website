Capabilities

Our platform spans app and newsletter development, audience growth, personalization, engagement, monetization, and publisher-advertiser networks — all proven at scale.

Seven bands below, in source order. Each carries what the capability is for, not a second name for it.

  1. 01

    App and newsletter development

    The owned surfaces a publisher controls outright, built and shipped as product rather than rented from a platform.

  2. 02

    Audience and subscriber growth

    Converting reach that is borrowed today into a list the publisher keeps, and keeping that list subscribing.

  3. 03

    Recommendation and personalization

    Ranking that can tell a returning reader from a first arrival, and answers each of them differently.

  4. 04

    Engagement and retention

    The work that starts after acquisition, where an owned audience is either compounded or quietly lost.

  5. 05

    Advertising technology

    The revenue layer underneath the audience: serving, yield, and the reporting that settles both.

  6. 06

    Content moderation

    Keeping an owned surface one that readers trust and advertisers are willing to appear on.

  7. 07

    Publisher-advertiser networks

    Demand and supply matched across publishers, so a single title is not left selling alone.

Mission

Empower media companies to own their audience, control their growth, and thrive in a changing media landscape.

We help publishers build direct audience relationships and strengthen the capabilities to grow, engage, retain, and monetize them.

Vision

Build the owned-audience growth infrastructure for the future of media.

We envision a future where publishers rely less on third-party distribution and have the technology, capabilities, and control to build sustainable growth on their own terms.
